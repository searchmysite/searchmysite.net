Discover the Independent Web

searchmysite.net is an independent blog search engine dedicated entirely to personal websites, 'digital gardens', and the 'small web'.

We index thousands of vetted, personal websites to help you find real content written by real people, completely ad-free and privacy-first. To get the most out of it, search for topics people might write about on their sites, such as travel diaries or technical guides. You should find the kinds of personal perspectives which can get a bit lost on the big search engines filled with their corporate marketing and blog spam. Pages with ad-free content are boosted in the results to discourage spam, and the entire platform is fully open source for greater transparency and community involvement.

Add Site Search to Your Website

Give your readers a fast, private way to explore your content. If your website is indexed with us, you can add a custom search box to your site using just one line of code, with no databases or servers required.

  • Free Tiers: A Basic listing is free forever and lets you search your indexed pages with results hosted on searchmysite.net, and Free Trial lets you try the Full listing features for a month without cost.
  • Premium Tier (£12/year): A Full listing keeps visitors on your own site with embedded results, and gives additional benefits such as more frequent re-indexing, on-demand re-indexing, and indexing significantly more content.

Prefer to run it yourself? The tool is fully open source and self-hostable.
